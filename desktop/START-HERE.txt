ARmedias Quantum
================

This folder is everything needed on a Windows PC with MetaTrader 5.
Put it anywhere (the Desktop is fine) and keep it in one piece.

  bin\          xaubridge.dll (the trading engine MetaTrader loads) and lab.exe
  mt5\          XauBridgeEA.mq5 (the MetaTrader expert) and its manual
  docs\         CUSTOM-STRATEGIES.md: adding strategies, the lab, live learning
  strategies\   the example strategy, a template for new ones
  python\       the price-history downloader

Double-click, in order
----------------------

0-DOWNLOAD-DATA.bat
    Downloads gold price history. Needs Python 3 (python.org; tick
    "Add python.exe to PATH" when installing). Ten years takes an hour or more.

1-INSTALL-INTO-MT5.bat
    Copies the engine and the expert into MetaTrader 5 and compiles the
    expert. If it says the expert did not compile, send the error lines to
    Claude. Close MetaTrader 5 before running it.

    Then, in MetaTrader 5:
      - Tools > Options > Expert Advisors: tick "Allow DLL imports"
      - open a gold chart on a DEMO account, drag XauBridgeEA onto it
      - InpStrategy = @champion  (trade whatever the lab picks)
      - InpDryRun   = true       (watch it first; set false to trade on demo)

2-RANK-STRATEGIES.bat
    Tests every strategy on the history with real costs and names a champion,
    or NONE. NONE is the usual answer and it is the right one: the champion has
    to beat what luck alone produces across every strategy ever tried. The
    full table opens when it finishes.

3-UPDATE-FROM-LIVE.bat
    Learns from the trades the expert has actually made: retires probable
    losers, promotes proven winners (40+ trades), and updates the champion.
    The expert switches to a new champion only when it holds no position.

4-SCHEDULE-DAILY-UPDATE.bat
    Runs step 3 every day at 23:30 by itself. 5-REMOVE-SCHEDULE.bat undoes it.

Safety
------
  - The expert refuses a REAL account unless InpAllowRealAccount is set.
    Do not set it before six weeks and 40+ trades on demo.
  - It halts and closes everything at a 4% daily loss or 8% drawdown, on the
    HALT button, or when MQL5\Files\XAU_STOP.txt exists. Only a person resumes.
  - No strategy here has yet passed its validation on real data.

Adding strategies
-----------------
See docs\CUSTOM-STRATEGIES.md. A new strategy is one file, added to the
project on GitHub; the next green CI run produces a new copy of this folder
with it inside.
